Owner management enquiries: Pipedrive and photo reminder
Authorised by Ricardo on 4 November 2026.

The public two-step form remains contact-first and photographs remain optional.
Every saved enquiry is reconciled with Pipedrive, including v3/legacy enquiries
stored since the form migration. No welcome or sales email is replayed by CRM
recovery. ActiveCampaign and Make are not reactivated.

New records use Portugal Active's existing Leads & Deals / Inbound routing,
owner22976488, pipeline1, stage1, status open. Existing contacts are reused by
exact email. All qualification answers, annual NET owner expectation, attribution
and current media are in one integration-owned note. Files are uploaded once per
content digest. Later changes update that note and the same opportunity, leaving
commercial notes, owner, stage, value and status under the team's control.

An uncertain creation response is held for reconciliation, not blindly repeated.
An existing opportunity or Leads Inbox item for the contact triggers review before
a new opportunity is created. Recovery decisions must be recorded administratively
in the durable operation ledger. Details: server/services/owner-crm/README.md.

The owner receives at most one automatic photo reminder: after48h from capture,
at least24h after the welcome was accepted, and after24h without recent changes.
The same private button opens the photo-only page; the full form is not repeated.
The email has the Portugal Active logo and is available in all10 form languages.
No reminder is sent once an image or photo link is present, the private link has
expired, the recipient changed, or the linked CRM deal is closed/archived/deleted.
The final check runs after local send pacing. An email already accepted by the
provider cannot be recalled if photos arrive during that final network request.
The website does not parse email replies. Commercial staff must handle replies;
closing/archiving the CRM deal prevents the remaining automatic reminder.

Queue rules remain durable across restart, with the same provider idempotency key.
Welcome and management notifications take priority over reminders. Confirmed quota
rejections defer instead of dropping the request. Unconfirmed deliveries are held
before the provider's deduplication window expires, for manual verification.

Resend remains on the user's chosen FREE plan. Its100-emails/day limit is shared
with other website mail.100 enquiries/day can generate more than100 emails/day;
the durable queue prevents loss but cannot promise immediate mail at that volume.
Pipedrive synchronization runs independently of that email quota.

Production feature controls:
  OWNER_CRM_ENABLED=true
  PIPEDRIVE_API_TOKEN=<production secret>
  PIPEDRIVE_OWNER_ID=22976488
  PIPEDRIVE_STAGE_ID=1
  OWNER_PHOTO_REMINDERS_ENABLED=true
  OWNER_AUTOMATION_FROM=2026-11-03T00:00:00Z
Optional OWNER_AUTOMATION_TEST_IDS explicitly allows only chosen synthetic UUIDs.
QA identities are otherwise excluded. DEV rejects operational credentials/flags.

Rollout: review and synthetic tests -> required CI -> main/production release ->
production-only environment configuration -> live schema/queue checks -> controlled
synthetic enquiry/replay/media/reminder -> reconcile all five genuine missing
enquiries and repeat the reconciliation checks -> remove temporary QA configuration.
Schema changes create three owner_crm tables only; existing enquiries are retained.

Rollback: set OWNER_CRM_ENABLED=false and OWNER_PHOTO_REMINDERS_ENABLED=false,
then redeploy the same verified application commit. This pauses the new work while
preserving saved requests, CRM mappings and the existing form/email workflow.
Do not delete or reset operation ledger rows to retry an uncertain remote create.

Health: /api/owner-enquiries/v4/health exposes booleans only, including CRM
configuration/attention and reminder enabled state. Review cases, stale retries,
or enquiries not synchronized after15min raise CRM attention. No personal fields,
private photo links or provider secrets are exposed by this endpoint.

Validation uses synthetic data and mocked providers locally/CI. Production smoke
tests are explicitly labelled PA-QA and use management@portugalactive.com only.
No customer data or operational credentials are connected to DEV.
