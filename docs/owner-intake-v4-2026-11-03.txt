Owner intake v4 — 3 November 2026

Requested by Ricardo: contact-first two-stage intake, optional photographs,
transactional welcome with a link to finish the SAME enquiry, measurement and
capacity for up to 100 enquiries/day. No sales portal or reminder campaign.

New API: /api/owner-enquiries/v4
POST /start persists contact, property type/locality, privacy acknowledgement,
public page context, optional consented campaign attribution, welcome and sales
notification in one transaction. Duplicate request UUID + identical input returns
the same reference and continuation token, with no additional email jobs.
GET/PATCH /draft, PUT/DELETE /files/:uuid and POST /complete require the random
256-bit bearer token. The email link carries it in the fragment. It expires after
30 days. Its lookup is hashed; the frozen private welcome outbox necessarily
retains the original link for reliable retries. Tokens/PII are never logged.
Email remains fixed after capture; other contact fields are editable inline.

Qualification and files update the original reference. Empty media is accepted;
an image or link is required to leave awaiting_photos, and commercial review must
still establish whether those images are accessible and sufficient. A PDF alone
does not imply photographs. Files are private database blobs, not public assets.
Uploads are binary and individually bounded (5 MiB each, 8 files/15 MiB active).
Removed file UUIDs cannot be revived by an in-flight PUT. Frozen notification
snapshots retain their referenced bytes; historical uploads are bounded too.

Captures and small updates have their own admission lane. Parsing counts actual
bytes even without Content-Length, with memory/concurrency budgets and explicit
Retry-After on 429. Legacy v3 remains supported with its more costly base64 bound.
Email delivery is asynchronous from a durable outbox, at most 2 concurrent jobs
and 15 MiB attachment bytes in progress. Provider idempotency keys and immutable
payloads protect ambiguous retries. Unconfirmed attempts require review before
the provider's idempotency window ends. Explicit quota rejections respect backoff.

Deployment is additive: four new owner_v4_* tables; no old lead tables removed or
rewritten. Deploy backend first, verify /v4/health, then publish the B2B Worker.
Rollback the B2B UI to its previous continuous-v3 version if needed; keep the v4
backend/outbox available for already-issued links and accepted enquiries.
Do not restore the old Worker that forwarded directly to ActiveCampaign/Make.

Validation uses synthetic data, mocked SQL/provider integrations and isolated
HTTP load tests. It must not load production credentials/data into local DEV.
Production reception tests are separate authorized administrative verification.
Render inspected for this release: 1 instance, 1 CPU / 2 GB; no plan upgrade.
Local load results are not production latency/capacity certification. Resend's
sending-only key cannot verify daily/monthly quotas; ensure allowance for welcome,
sales notifications, completion updates and the existing B2C email traffic.
